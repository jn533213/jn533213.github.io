Skills
======
* Scientific & Statistical Analysis
  * 6+ years experience using Python and Ubuntu. Used both daily for determining spatio-temporal data analysis methods including empirical orthogonal functions (including common variants), high-pass and low-pass filters, linear regression, and self-organizing maps. Highly skilled in using NumPy, SciPy, and matplotlib Python packages. Proficient in Jupyter notebooks.
  * 6+ years of experience in GIS figure and graphic creation using Python, including spatial figures and animations containing maps. Skilled in efficiently and effectively creating scientific figures that successfully illustrate important research findings. Contributed figures for other colleagues on multiple presentations and publications.
  * Creating and maintaining multiple published large oceanographic data products, with the aim of distributing analysis-ready products to the wider public. Extensive experience creating the underlying methodology and delivering large-scale spatio-temporal data products using Python and Ubuntu in NetCDF file format.
  * Researching and implementing multiple levels of quality assurance and control with specific methodologies tailored to each data product. Various statistical analyses are used to better flag potential problem measurements, and appropriate metadata is provided to ensure that end users are properly informed.
* Community Engagement & Field Work
  * Participating as the lead reporting physical oceanographer in multi-stakeholder meetings, involving federal, provincial, local, private, and Indigenous partners. Findings presented at the meeting are used to help inform future federal environmental policy. Lead author of the accompanying yearly physical oceanographic technical report.
  * Leading the Newfoundland region ocean glider program. This involves the coordinating of deployment and recovery missions with local partners in small coastal communities. Regular communication and scientific contributions are also made with other regions’ ocean glider programs.
  * Participating in multiple field-going oceanographic missions per year as part of the Atlantic Zone Monitoring Program. This involves the monitoring of physical and biogeochemical processes throughout the Newfoundland and Labrador region and requires being far offshore for 3-4 weeks at time in extreme weather conditions.
  * Familiar with a variety of oceanographic instrumentation including CTDs, Niskins, passive acoustics (hydrophones), ADCPs, gliders, and a variety of mooring-based instruments.
  * Presenting research findings to large and academically diverse audiences. Skilled in presenting research findings to groups with varying levels of background knowledge on a particular subject in both an oral and written format.
  * Highly skilled at working within multidisciplinary groups, including regular communication with regional and international partners. Was the lead author for a published data product containing almost all ocean profile measurements (over 800,000) taken in the Northwest Atlantic and Arctic from 1912 to 2024 , which involved cooperating with over 10 independent organizations, and  included accompanying configuration and quality control documentation.
  * Providing end users with data products that are formatted for their particular needs, including in NetCDF and csv formats. Routinely providing support to end users to help resolve any issues that may arise.
* Project Management
  * CSAS – Canadian Science Advisory Secretariat
    * Lead reporting physical oceanographer for the Newfoundland and Labrador region, providing climate advice on the setting of yearly fishery quotas.
    * Engage with multiple stakeholders on culturally and economically important species.
  * ICES Climate Report – International Council for the Exploration of the Sea
    * Collaborating on the ICES Baffin Bay climate report using data products that I created.
  * COD-REMAP – Climate and ocean dynamics informing resource management and adaptation policy
    * Exploring how the abundance and distribution of fish species on the Northwest Atlantic are linked to changing physical and biological ocean conditions over the last 2000 years.
    * Providing and communicating physical oceanographic results to group members, along with writing the ‘Climate’ section of the published paper.
